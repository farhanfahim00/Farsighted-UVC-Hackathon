# Data notice

The following are provided by Certivity **for use in the Certivity hackathon only**:

- everything under `cases/` and `subgoal/` (specifications, drawings, expected outputs, requirement lists)
- `regulations/index.json`
- the held-out inputs that are released during the event

You may use them to take part in the hackathon: to build, test and submit your solution. You may not
redistribute them, or use them for any other purpose, without Certivity's written permission.

The regulation PDFs that `tools/fetch_regulations.py` downloads are official documents of the United
Nations Economic Commission for Europe (UNECE); `regulations/index.json` gives the original address of
each. They are hosted temporarily for convenience and so that everyone works from exactly the same files.
The download links stop working after the event.
