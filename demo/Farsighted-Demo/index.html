04 / Under the hood

Automation with auditability.

A pragmatic architecture: deterministic document processing for traceable evidence, and model-assisted reasoning only where interpretation is needed.

Main solution

Product to obligations
01 Parse product specification and identify component functions
02 Retrieve indexed UN Regulation sections and applicability evidence
03 Model-assisted clause screening and requirement extraction
04 Atomicity, relevance review and citation checks
05 Schema-validated, version-specific JSON submission

Regulation-change solution

Version A to B
01 Compare old and new regulation provisions
02 Match existing obligations against corresponding new passages
03 Identify independently testable additions, modifications and removals
04 Preserve exact source evidence, clause and page
05 Validate proposed changes against the submission schema
Designed for human sign-off
The agent accelerates research and creates a reviewable engineering artifact. It does not replace a responsible engineer's or homologation specialist's final applicability judgment.
