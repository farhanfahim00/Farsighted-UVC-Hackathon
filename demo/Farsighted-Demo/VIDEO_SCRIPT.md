## Video checklist

1. Use a real generated main submission - not the organizer's `expected.json`.
2. Make sure the Requirements view is populated before recording.
3. Keep the recording shorter than 2:00. Recommended target: 1:50-1:55.
4. Use the Windows Snipping Tool screen recorder, Clipchamp or your existing recording software.
5. Export at 1080p when available; audio must be intelligible.
6. Upload to YouTube as **unlisted** (or an organizer-approved alternative). Copy the share link and test it in an incognito window.
